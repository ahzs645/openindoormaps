Harrods stairs, escalators and elevators — source comparison and repair
2026-10-01. UNBC excluded.

Implemented locally

The source's 53 stair, 104 escalator and 83 elevator footprints are preserved
at their original positions and rotations, including polygon holes. An
independent comparison against merged raw tiles verifies less than 0.1 mm
boundary deviation from coordinate rounding. Most appearance differences
came from added decoration: 743 generated tread/chevron/cross lines and
dashed outlines, plus bright fills. Those are removed for this source.
Connector fills, opacity, original sprite icons and icon collision behavior
now follow the captured light_osm.json style. Other venues retain their styles.

The prior proximity grouping merged parallel escalator lanes into 9 banks.
One-to-one matching across adjacent floors now retains 24 separate multi-floor
lane chains; these are inferred chains, not a verified count of operating
escalators. Captured landing pairs take priority over footprint overlap.
Unmatched footprints remain visible without fabricated floor links.
The 10 stair chains and 6 elevator banks remain.

Each adjacent-floor escalator flight now has its own connection ID, so two
flights generate two boarding steps and expose the intermediate landing.
Vertical route lines are split on connection ID and carry their actual
boarding/arrival floors, including reverse trips. Consecutive hops within
the same elevator remain one ride.

Source taxonomy transition times are used: 45 seconds per escalator flight,
60 seconds per stair floor, and a fixed 120-second elevator ride regardless
of floors crossed. Elevator entry/exit each cost 60 seconds, with zero extra
hop cost; ride_time_seconds identifies this model explicitly. The displayed
summary uses the same full-ride time. Other venues keep their existing model.

Only the observed Lower Ground -> Ground escalator flight is known upward.
The direction constraint is not propagated to its unobserved upper flights.
Source taxonomy says escalators are unidirectional, but does not identify
up/down orientation. Unobserved flights still use the explicitly unknown
two-way routing fallback. They must not be presented as verified direction.

Verification

9 geometry unit tests passed, including separate parallel lanes, observed
pairing priority, unmatched lanes and missing-floor gaps. Independent raw
tile comparison checked all 240 footprints, fills, landing containment,
adjacent floor links, travel times and accessibility flags.

87 real-engine Harrods trips passed: 3 captured reference paths, both
directions at all 38 emitted stair links, and 8 normal/accessibility venue
trips. 74 landing trips actually used stairs. Two additional isolated
multi-flight checks verified instructions and intermediate-floor rendering
in both directions. Captured connector landings remain within 2.5 cm of
the observed endpoints. Unreachable destinations still clear previous paths.

All 63,402 horizontal graph segments and the tested walking routes avoid
the original obstacle cores using the existing 4 cm wall/6 cm virtual
obstacle precision tolerances. No obstacle tolerances were relaxed.
POI and obstacle export contents are unchanged by this connector repair.

40 sampled trips in each of City Mall, Harrods, Mappedin Mall, Bowie State
and Eaton Centre passed independent routing cost, floor, accessibility and
hop checks (200 cases). TypeScript and targeted ESLint checks passed.
8 existing browser tests passed; the added stair preview test also passed:
boarding focuses Lower Ground, the next step focuses Ground, and accessible
navigation replaces stairs with an elevator.

Reference walking-path fidelity is unchanged: 93.5%, 100%, and 73.9% of
sampled points within 2 m of the three captured source routes. The Door 3
to Sushi route still uses an alternative aisle for part of its path. The
full vendor navigation graph and most operating directions are unavailable.
