Harrods interior display and aisle angles
2026-10-01. Continues the earlier connector repair; UNBC excluded.

Problem and change

The renderer drew each department as an opaque 2.5 m block. These roofs
masked the interior walls and floor surfaces in the pitched map. The import
also omitted the source walkway/circulation polygons from the visible plan,
although routing already used them.

Departments now render as flat surfaces, with the captured style's department
colours where defined. The map exports all 34 walkway and 53 circulation
polygons, and renders the 367 source wall polygon parts at the original
0.75 m height. Polygon holes and positions are preserved. Unit/corridor
heights are explicit properties; other venues keep their existing defaults.
Walls have their own extrusion layer so they do not receive shop click/hover
behavior. Flat surfaces skip the redundant extrusion pass to avoid striped
depth conflicts, and flat departments retain click/hover interaction. Room
selection now matches POIs by floor and building before polygon containment;
a regression check covers overlapping stacked rooms in two buildings. The initial Harrods zoom is closer (17.5 desktop, 16.5 mobile).

This reveals the rooms and departments present in the Harrods source.
Harrods has large open retail departments; it does not have the hospital's
many small clinical rooms. No new rooms or partitions are invented.

The triangle navigation mesh could produce small diagonal zigzags. The
Harrods importer now infers a principal axis from long aisle boundaries
on each floor (roughly 44 degrees for most floors, 66 degrees on Sixth).
It offers right-angle links within public aisle polygons, including around
corners where a diagonal would enter a wall. Both legs must pass the same
walkable-space and obstacle checks as every other graph edge. Existing
triangle links and narrow-door topology remain available.

A diagonal preference cost (0.6 maximum added multiplier in public aisles)
favours the building's axes, rather than compass north/south. It affects
route selection only; displayed distance/time still use actual walking.
It is a preference, not a promise that every leg is perpendicular: curved
aisles, other wings, door approaches and department destinations can still
need diagonal legs. Other venues do not enable this option.

Measured result

Across the same 87 engine route cases, including stair landing trips:
- Walking distance aligned within 3 degrees of a floor axis: 58.6% -> 64.4%.
- Turns over 15 degrees: 217 -> 155.
- Such turns with an adjacent leg under 2 m: 182 -> 95 (48% fewer).

The three captured connector sequences and exact boarding/arrival nodes
remain correct. Reference walking-route results:
- Lower Ground -> Third: 116.81 m, 93.4% of samples within 2 m of source.
- Mini Super Brands 3 -> Chocolate Hall: 144.04 m, 100% within 2 m.
- Door 3 -> Sushi: 119.56 m, 100% within 2 m (previously 73.9%).
The Door 3 alternative-aisle issue reported in the earlier audit is resolved
for this captured example. These three examples do not establish parity
with the vendor's complete navigation graph.

Verification

all visible interior layers, flat departments and low walls. Boundary
rounding tolerance is 0.1 mm; no routing obstacle tolerances were changed.

python3 scripts/verify-harrods-connectors.py still passes all 240 footprints
and emitted connector metadata. The fixed ride time and known direction
from the earlier repair remain. Unknown escalator directions remain unknown.

10 geometry tests pass, including a rotated open aisle whose chosen path
uses actual right-angle legs instead of triangle diagonals. 87 Harrods
engine route cases pass (76 using stairs), including accessibility and
unreachable-target behavior. All 81,777 horizontal graph edges and tested
walking paths avoid original source obstacle cores. POI and blocker exports
are unchanged. 200 samples across five non-UNBC venues pass independent
cost/floor/accessibility checks. Nine browser tests, TypeScript and targeted
ESLint checks pass. Sampled Harrods routing median 14 ms, maximum 51 ms.
