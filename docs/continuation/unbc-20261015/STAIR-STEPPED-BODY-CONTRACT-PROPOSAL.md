# Proposed original-native stepped body contract

Status: source-only prototype; production qualification remains unchanged and all 28 held connections remain disabled. The candidate compiler already running does not include this proposal.

The existing `NativeSourceStairBody` delegates to the flat projected-strip width predicate. That predicate rejects vertical segments and requires continuous positive flat support across each riser. Real step movement needs a distinct contract. Replacing the source coordinates, joining UV endpoints with an epsilon, or labelling uncertain space as floor would not establish that contract.

## Source inputs and mandatory gates

A proposed `kind: "original-native-stepped-support"`, version 2 body variant must consume these independently checked inputs. It must not silently weaken version 1.

1. **Actual source ownership and complete body census.** The compiler constructs the private context from actual RVT bytes and verified embedded schema. The original stair/run/definition ownership and occurrence placement must agree. Every declared physical Face/Edge/Loop/primitive and every source branch remains accounted for. Closed source topology and complete conservative material cells are required; numerical UV/endpoint disagreements remain explicit bounded operands. This does not assert that independently evaluated coordinates form one exact sewn prism.
2. **Actual walking roles and supported stations.** Persisted physical FaceHist binds each physical walking face to its original StairsTreadInfo index. Visit every index in order at the physical face's original plane height. The full two-foot lateral station lies inside guaranteed inner physical support. For a normal native tread, it also lies within the finite intended extent derived from the original `m_startLine` / `m_endLine`, oriented by the original `m_riserDir`. The intent extent is a path constraint; it is never substituted for physical support.
3. **Explicit source riser adjacency.** The original named `m_startRiser.m_specifiedIdx` / `m_endRiser.m_specifiedIdx`, original ordered riser loops and exact finite boundary line controls must establish each consecutive step. Preserve source begin/end-riser flags and base/top indices. Opaque EdgeHist opcodes are not interpreted as sewing instructions. Do not infer a missing riser by matching nearby geometry or equal counts.
4. **Stepped trajectory.** A rise uses the next physical tread plane height at a supported source station, followed by travel to the next supported station. That horizontal transfer must properly cross the original finite riser across the entire conservative two-foot lateral strip. Named lower/upper lines must match the corresponding ordered riser in exact plan controls and preserve independent source Z values and begin/end flags. A lift need not occur on the riser line; the exact-line vertical diagnostic retains real own-riser collisions and cannot be waived. Source-indexed air/step transfers retain every actual physical gap and source uncertainty operand. Original finite tread intent bounds the transition together with the physical source operands; this does not create positive flat floor across risers. Same-tread/same-height movement still requires continuous full-width guaranteed physical support, so a turning platform cannot cross its notch or a real hole.
5. **Both original terminals.** Independently read the actual lower and upper named Floor/GElement records, their complete source material and all slab holes. Preserve original logical graph coordinates. A distinct version 2 attachment may bind that served-level point to an actual physical support point at exactly the same XY and the original named trimmed face height. The actual source run `m_stairsId` and symbol `m_hostId` must establish explicit child-to-parent ownership; preserve the original parent `StairsElement.m_runsAndLandings` even when empty, without inventing reciprocal membership. The parent base/top level and the floor's associated level must establish the named served-level relation. Keep both heights and their exact difference; do not assert exact 3D contact when they differ. The physical foot trajectory starts on the actual face, and no synthetic walking segment connects a nominal level point through slab material. Check full-width inner floor support and the original begin/end riser. A bottom-to-top diagonal that intersects the slab is not an acceptable substitute for a step.
6. **Complete full-body clearance.** Sweep every horizontal and vertical transfer through the entire person-height demand, retaining exact tangent and raised-material contacts. Check all original owned upper flights/landings and all foreign material. Use cap-bounded source cores plus every localized side/endpoint uncertainty cell; do not spread a corner residual over the entire cap or delete it. A narrow ankle band, the old diagonal census, a blanket owned-stair exemption or cached unknown source completeness cannot satisfy this gate. The active navigation profile is two feet of transverse walking width and six feet of overhead clearance during source-indexed stair motion, plus full two-by-two-foot standing caps at actual flat landings and turns. A rigid two-by-two-foot footprint at every approximately eleven-inch tread is a separate gait/biomechanics model; the active route proof must state its profile explicitly and must not claim a general human-body certificate.
7. **Source/compiler/runtime agreement.** Only after every source gate passes may the compiler produce a new prepared variant bound to the model, schema, exact source frame hashes, physical profiles, station/riser sequence, terminal floors and material/clearance geometry. The runtime must consume the same discriminated variant and reject stale/missing geometry, changed access, changed source indices, a copied incoming evidence label or a fallback to the flat predicate. Preserve entry/exit ownership, one-way rules and normal public routing; stairs remain unavailable to step-free routing.

No existing production/runtime contract has been edited for this proposal. Passing one source helper does not authorize a caller to assert the other gates.

## Required negative controls before integration

- Missing, duplicated, out-of-order or skipped physical tread/riser indices; mismatched native finite boundary controls; an unsupported curve or source branch.
- A station outside either the physical cap or its normal native intended extent, even when a report count matches.
- An unsupported same-height platform crossing, a slab hole, or an endpoint shifted to the wrong side of a floor opening.
- Copied/forged source context or certificate labels; source/model/schema/frame/geometry changes after preparation.
- A raised obstacle missed by an ankle-only band; exact six-foot head tangency; an owned upper flight; a positive retained uncertainty contact.
- Missing or stale foreign census, a changed drawing-reference decision, an unsupported original slab branch, or a general owned-element exemption.
- Public access and one-way refusals, every step-free refusal, and actual calculator routes in both directions through a newly enabled edge.

The new pure exact finite-riser checker reports proper full-width crossings for all22 internal risers and both terminals on copied actual operands. Its source wrapper separately reads named lines from the opaque RVT context; this supplies no physical riser, footprint, terminal or route approval. Negative controls reject short finite lines, a point-only crossing, endpoint-only contact, parallel traces and a missing lateral track.

The source prototype already tests real source extraction, role/census omissions and duplicates, unforgeable source handles, actual physical support, concave/holed support, shifted stations, retained uncertainty, original named riser indices and skipped-riser rejection.
