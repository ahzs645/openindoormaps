## How nativeIndoorEnvelopes is derived
Reviter only validates/hashes/indexes it (`lib/reviter/native-indoor-envelopes.ts`); `indoor-pipeline.ts` verifies and copies it; `native-circulation-geometry.ts` intersects walking floors and threshold halves with envelope parts within 0.05 ft of the level elevation. The original generator (and that of `nativeMaterialSections`) is not recoverable. The authored envelope behaves like native floor ∩ (wall material ∪ space closed at EVERY cut height); cuts +0.1/+1/+4/+8/+10 ft (fractional levels +0.1 and +4). Barrier = production cutter categories (walls, curtain panels -2000171, mullions -2000170, columns) + physical door footprints (enclosure only) + applied seals/frame returns + wall-position repairs + production junction repairs (end caps ≤24.4 mm, corner wedges ≤12.2 mm). Approximate curtain-host footprints excluded; windows (-2000014) not in cutter categories. Calibration: rebuilt barrier reproduces the authored envelope (37 sq ft slivers, 0/97 rows differ) and correctly excludes parapet-only terraces. Enclosure is per connected free space, so one gap anywhere drops a whole doorless corridor network.
