## Evidence limits and next decisions

This review read the checksummed visitor graph/metadata and encoded face identities, compared them with the shared-guide selector, and traced the owning compiler predicates. It did not rebuild native geometry, visually inspect the complete RVT/GLB, replay each exact face's physical support, qualify new routes or determine public/step-free access. The large native-cache split inputs were not needed to establish these selection causes and were not downloaded for this review.

The current source graph's omissions are explained. Physical completeness still needs a decision on the four unmatched zero-walk identities, any shared-identity fragment that should carry a real entrance/connector, the mixed-owner shared-guide scope, and the intended use of the four native levels. Preserve existing doors, rooms, obstacles, holes and transition states while gathering that evidence. No source repair is established solely by this inventory.
