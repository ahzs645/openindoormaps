# Original threshold completion work

These are source-bound consumers and read-only original-source replays. They do not enable a doorway, edit the master, grant public access or establish a campus release.

## What is implemented

- A complete current curtain cell/member reader matches original native generator panels, cells, face tags and coedges to all original saved graphics. Original control/reference branches stay in its ledger.
- The material-container consumer follows the wall's own native type ID to its original curtain wall type. - The exact filled-sill consumer retains all six original filled faces, twelve edges, loop/twin roles, original face orientation, two physical cap planes and the full original affine. It partitions the whole two-foot strip into floor/cap regions and two original oblique risers, and requires both complete endpoint standing squares.
- Scoped native floor support retains every original hole, curve, twin/join uncertainty and the native 1437 acceleration-chain ledger. It proves one connected complete footprint away from every source boundary uncertainty with original outer/hole windings. The global floor-reference validity gate remains unchanged; the original 1505065 outer-ring self-intersection is still refused globally.
- The combined supported-material consumer re-reads the floor, assembly and every unknown physical source envelope. An unknown row can be replaced only by its genuine source-constructor enclosure proved disjoint from the entire walking/standing demand. Closed side/head contacts remain vetoes. Bounds prove exclusions only.
- The strict native curtain-door definition reader retains all current source constructor, parameter, reference-face and four finite profile-line operands. It preserves the original tiny direction terms and separate join endpoints. Width/height, a door category and flip controls do not authorize operation or erase saved leaf/handle material.

The nominal 50 mm sill rise is retained exactly, including its original binary rational value slightly above mathematical 50 mm. No new epsilon or maximum-rise/accessibility waiver is introduced. Production demand uses six feet above the highest cap across the complete strip, plus both standing interiors. Historical seven-foot reports remain conservative diagnostics.
