Indoor floor preparation worker — 2026-10-02

Rooms, wall junctions, room boundaries, lower floor context, stair ground
occlusion and stair apertures are now prepared in a module Web Worker.
MapLibre source uploads, labels, camera updates and rendering remain on the
main thread. Source records and routing geometry are unchanged.

Each mounted map owns a worker and a bounded cache of 12 prepared floor /
building / geometry settings combinations per immutable dataset. Cancelling
an unfinished request terminates the worker, while completed floor results
remain reusable. Request IDs reject obsolete replies. Imported or edited
datasets get separate caches. Errors show a retry action; there is no
synchronous polygon-processing fallback that would freeze the page again.
During preparation the old floor sources are cleared, picking is removed,
and the floor selector remains interactive. 2D/3D camera toggles reuse the
same prepared footprints.

The manual floor/building selectors retain selected room/edge IDs and fit
the new floor without using that selection's bounds. Previously, a selected
Library room such as 05-247 could override the campus bounds on returning
to Floor 2. Floor browsing now keeps its place card and restores its
highlight when that floor is visible, while showing the floor overview.
Search and direction following retain their intentional focus behavior.
The Whole floor review control also ignores the selected room's bounds.
