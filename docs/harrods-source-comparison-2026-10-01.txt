Indoor map source comparison — 2026-10-01

Verdict
Harrods walking routes are physically invalid against the captured source
layout. The earlier navigation audit checked shortest paths and UI behavior
within the supplied graphs; it did not establish fidelity to the building.
UNBC remains excluded.

Evidence
Reused the original route's exact start/end coordinates and floors in the
current IndoorDirections engine. Normal graph snapping still applies.
Compared each horizontal segment with the actual source wall polygon
material, preserving holes and merging tile fragments. Removed the small
per-floor coordinate separation used by the importer. Eroded walls by 10 cm
and counted only segments overlapping more than 25 cm, to avoid boundary
rounding false positives.

Across the Harrods store graph, 1,590 of 3,656 ordinary walking segments
enter source wall material. 141 enter source virtual obstacles. These
counts overlap and should not be added.

The drawn wall geometry is faithful: per-floor symmetric difference from
source wall material is only 0.001–0.020 percent (coordinate rounding).
This points to the route generation, rather than a map alignment problem.

Cause
scripts/port-pointr-harrods.py:644 supplies only units smaller than 200 m2 as
routing obstacles. Source walls are drawn as areas and omitted from the
obstacle input. Virtual obstacles and walkway layers are also omitted.
Floor envelopes used for sampling are bounding rectangles rather than
verified walkable floor boundaries.

scripts/port_vendors_common.py:474 joins disconnected graph components
without obstacle visibility checks. At line 522, POI attachment falls back
to the nearest hub regardless of obstacles. Both can reintroduce invalid
links even if obstacle-aware generation is added elsewhere.

Elevators and escalators
The three captured comparisons agree on connector type and floor-change
sequence (one is same-floor). Elevator/escalator footprints originate from
source tiles, but grouping them into shafts is inferred from proximity.
Except for one captured up-only escalator, travel directions default to
two-way. Agreement on these samples does not validate all shafts, travel
directions, access restrictions, or the walking approaches.
Harrods car park entrances and outdoor connectors are also inferred.

Repair direction
Prefer obtaining the complete vendor walking graph, rather than inferring
it from a few captured routes. If it is unavailable, rebuild within actual
walkable geometry, enforce walls/virtual obstacles on every edge including
component joins and POI attachments, and retain disconnected areas when no
valid passage is known. Recheck captured routes for zero wall crossings.
Do not treat a single captured polyline as a complete building network.
Use Eaton Centre/Bowie State for further source-based demonstrations while
Harrods is corrected; their added POI spurs still need physical validation.
